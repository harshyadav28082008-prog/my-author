BEYOND THE KNOWN WORLD

One of a Kind

The Skyship is a unique vessel capable of travelling through the sky. No other known creation serves the same purpose, and its existence remains unknown to the wider world.

It does not rely on magic to fly. Nor can its presence be detected through magic.
