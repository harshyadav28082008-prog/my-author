Blue Nation

Where the sea shapes the land, and magic follows the flow of water.

The Lower Dragon Jaw — a coastal realm on the North-East Landmass.

01 · THE REALM

Land & Sea

REGION

Lower Dragon Jaw

Located along the coastal and bay regions of the North-East Landmass.

TERRAIN

A Coastal Landscape

Cliffs overlook the sea, beaches trace the coastline, and maritime valleys shape the interior.

CLIMATE

Temperate Maritime

Mild winters and cool summers define the seasonal character of the region.

WEATHER

A Land of Rain & Rivers

Heavy rainfall throughout the year keeps rivers consistently full, creating numerous waterfalls. Moist equatorial winds bring frequent sea storms, though these are rarely destructive.

02 · THE FLOW OF POWER

Blue Magic

The magic of Blue Nation is associated with water and its natural movement.

MAGICAL AFFINITY

Fluidity. Flow. Water.

The water-rich diet and culture of Blue Nation influence the character of its magic. Blue Magic glows a deep blue and is associated with fluidity and flow.
