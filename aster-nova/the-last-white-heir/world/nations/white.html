A Land of White

Geography, climate, and the nature of White Magic.

01 / GEOGRAPHY

Between Sea and Stone

The White Nation occupies a northern island and a small stretch of mainland coast on the north-eastern landmass. Its capital, Aurellion, stands on the island.

Region North-East Landmass
Latitude Approximately 40°
Terrain Cliffs · Fjords · Cold Coast
02 / CLIMATE

The Northern Winds

A sub-polar oceanic climate shapes life in the kingdom. Winters are long and harsh, snowfall is frequent, and cold fog drifts inland from the northern current.

  • Short, cool, windy summers
  • Frequent storms where warm equatorial currents meet polar winds
  • Aurora-like magical lights visible at night

06 · HISTORY

The Day the Crown Shattered

A birthday celebration became a turning point in the history of the White Nation.

✦
THE ROYAL TRAGEDY

The Castle's Destruction

On the day of Caelis's birthday celebration, a magical explosion destroyed the castle. Caelis and his siblings had already been taken to safety.

King Alaric and Queen Seraphina were killed. The military commander lost an arm, while many nobles died. The bodies of several others were never found.

The Royal Library was also attacked during the incident.

THE SUCCESSION

A New King

In the aftermath of the tragedy, Caelis declared himself the new king of the White Nation.

◇

Some details of this event remain unknown. The circumstances behind the attack are not fully recorded here.
