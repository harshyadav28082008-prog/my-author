WHY THE CROWNS GATHERED

A Council Meant to Protect.
A Power That Began to Consume.

The nine kings discovered that the Nexus Council was misusing its authority and gradually consuming the power of their nations.

What had once been entrusted to the Council had become a source of growing imbalance. The kings formed the Royal Summit to stand together, challenge that authority, and reclaim the power they had lost.

THEIR COMMON PURPOSE Overpower the Nexus Council and reclaim their nations' lost authority.

THE ROYAL PROTOCOL

Rules of the Summit

01

Three Crowns Must Agree

A summit date can be agreed upon only when at least three kings consent to it.

02

Ten Days' Notice

Once a date is agreed upon, the other kings must be informed approximately ten days before the meeting.

03

Every Crown Must Be Represented

All nine kings must attend. If a king cannot attend personally, a representative of equal status must take their place.

04

Two Champions Only

Each king may bring no more than two champions. They must remain outside the summit chamber.

05

Nine Doors. Nine Chairs.

The chamber has nine doors and nine chairs, one reserved for each of the nine kings.

06

A Place Unknown to Humanity

No human knows the summit's location. The carriages and their horses alone know the route that leads there.

07

Equal Time to Reach the Unknown

Regardless of where a journey begins, every traveler takes the same amount of time to reach the summit's location.
