THE NAME

Born from Ash. Bound by Oath.

I

Ashen

The aftermath of destruction. A name shaped by the fall of the White Nation and the imbalance that followed.

II

Covenant

An oath-bound collective, not a gathering devoted to chaos. Its name speaks of a shared conviction and a binding purpose.

Dangerous in its opposition. Not defined by evil.

CORE IDEOLOGY

Who Has the Right to Wield Power?

The Ashen Covenant rejects the belief that power should be controlled by distant authorities.

◇

Against the Nexus Council

They oppose the Council's authority and question its right to regulate power.

◇

Against Aetherion Concord Academy

They oppose the Academy's system of strict magical classification and bans.

◇

Against Suppression

They believe the Council protects established elites while suppressing those who possess power outside its accepted boundaries.

KNOWN HISTORY

What the Ashes Remember

AFTER THE FALL

Born from Imbalance

The Ashen Covenant emerged after the fall of the White Nation, amid the imbalance and corruption that followed.

Despite the name and the circumstances of its formation, the Covenant was not responsible for the White Nation's destruction.

◇

THE TURNING POINT

A Defeated Leader.
A Pledged Loyalty.

Caelis Lysandriel defeated the leader of the Ashen Covenant and brought the entire Underworld under his control.

Following the defeat, the Covenant's leader pledged loyalty to Caelis.
