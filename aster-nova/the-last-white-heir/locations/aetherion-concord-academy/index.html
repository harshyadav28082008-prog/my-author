CENTRAL ISLAND · INSTITUTIONAL RECORD

Aetherion Concord Academy

Where the next generation prepares to shape the world.

An institution of learning, discipline, and preparation for those destined to serve their nations.

01 / PURPOSE

An Education Before Authority

Every royal and noble is required to study at Aetherion Concord Academy and obtain its passing qualification. Only after passing can they take an official position within their respective nation.

The Academy's purpose extends beyond ordinary education. It prepares the next generation to understand the responsibilities that accompany authority.
