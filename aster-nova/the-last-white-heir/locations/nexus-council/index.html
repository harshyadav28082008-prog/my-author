01 / THE INSTITUTION

An Authority Beyond Borders

The Nexus Council serves as an international governing body responsible for matters concerning magic and the rules that shape its place in the world.

It also serves as an international rule-making and enforcement authority, addressing matters that extend beyond the jurisdiction of any single nation.

02 / MAGICAL AUTHORITY

The Governance of Magic

The Council oversees the discovery of magic, its use, and the rules governing it. Matters concerning magical practice and regulation fall within its authority.

03 / ROYAL SUCCESSION

The Right to Rule

The Council determines how a new king or queen should be selected and the conditions under which they may become the ruler of their nation.

Royal succession is therefore not solely a matter confined to the royal family.
