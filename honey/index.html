02 UPCOMING

Temporary Title · Romance · School Life · Friendship

From the Corner

In a class where a small group of students regularly occupies the highest ranks, one student quietly remains near the top.

He is always second. Somehow, almost nobody knows it.

The third-ranked girl knows the truth, but the class has built its own misunderstanding because he never bothers to correct it.

She is also a close friend of the introvert girl he secretly likes.

And his bench mate — the student who always ranks first — already knows exactly who his best friend likes.

The title is temporary. The story is not.
